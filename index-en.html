The Group’s identity

An entrepreneurial group.A shared vision.

Founded and chaired by Imed Riahi, Groupe Smart Concept structures and develops companies, agencies and brands across complementary sectors.

Its model combines shared leadership, autonomous local teams and pooled resources. This structure allows each business to retain its expertise while benefiting from the Group’s strength, methods and momentum.

03 / A message from the President
The founder’s vision

Build.Unite. Move forward.

“A shared ambition, complementary expertise and clear accountability at every stage.”

Since Groupe Smart Concept was founded, our ambition has remained unchanged: to bring complementary expertise together around one simple principle—honour our commitments and create lasting value.

Our growth in France, Morocco, Tunisia and Martinique is driven by hands-on leaders, specialist brands and a shared vision. We move forward methodically, staying close to the realities of each market while preparing our next locations in Europe, the Middle East and the United States.

This portfolio presents more than a group: it presents an organisation capable of envisioning, structuring and supporting ambitious projects, with an accountable point of contact at every stage.

Let’s build what comes next—together.
